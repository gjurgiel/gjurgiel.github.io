6.6220 is the garduate level power electronics class at MIT taught by Dave Perault. It's a class I very much enjoyed taking and is an overview of many topics in power electronics. As a final design project for the class we has to make a design for reletivly simple mid-range power buck converter. We had to do all the design work inclusing component selection, filter design, magnetics design, thermal analysis, and conteroller design. Due to time constrainst we were not required to physically buiold this circuit or any PCB design. We did have to prove our design fullfilled certain effeicientcy, ripple, and trnsisnrt requirments through reasonable analysis and simulation. 

The design I came up with appears to work well acording to my analysis. My design is not particularly special in any way, but the processes tught me a lot about how to go about designing my own power converters, particularly in the importance of considering design aspects outside the main power swithcing cell and singlmindedly following effecientcy. A full write-up of my design is linked [here]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/2025-05-12-6622-design-project-files/6_622_Design_Project.pdf).
