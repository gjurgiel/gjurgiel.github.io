Professor Dave Perrault only offers the Special Topics in Power Electronics class every couple of years. By chance I happened to take to normal power class the semester prior so I had the opportunity to take 6.S892. The class is grab bag of different topics largely dictated by what Dave wants his grad students to be familiar with. We spent time looking at various less circuit analysis techniques such as topological and time reversal duality. We went much deeper into going magnetics design, looking at more complex magnetic structures and losses. We also spent a lot of time on RF and resonant topologies and as well as piezoelectric converts. Notably absent were switched capacitor converters likely because this is an area of specialization for another professor, Sam Coday, and has been left for classes and lectures by her.

Much like the previous power class, there was a final design project, however this project was left much more open ended. I decided to make power converter that would take single phase AC and converter it to three phase AC. This was in part chosen because I have a three phase induction motor that I would like to try and run using this, but also because I was able to make a design touching on many aspects of power design to get some familiarity with it.
