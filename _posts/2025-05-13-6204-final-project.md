Analog Electronics Laboratory or 6.2040 (previously 6.101) is approximately what it sounds like. It is essentially an extension of the introductory circuits class with a focus on analog transistor circuits and a heavy lab component. The class gives students proficiency with basic electronics lab equipment doing a lot or circuit breadboard  to put theory into practice. Because of the history of the class it spends most of it's time with BJTs as opposed to FETs.

The class has a large final group project components taking up half the semester. Working in a group with Kat Jander and Ellie Bultena, we designed and built a fairly simple analog modular synthesizer. As a novelty notes were clocked off a EKG we had built earlier in the class.

I was responsible for the design of the synthesizers sequencer, Low Frequency Oscillator, and ADSR Envelope Generator. These all largely just generate low frequency control signals to modulate audio frequency filters and oscillators made by others.
