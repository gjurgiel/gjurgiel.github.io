*There are two ways to make a design robust to failure: exhaustively test it under all possible conditions until you understand every possible failure mode and how to respond to it, or to make it so it can fail in as few ways as possible all of which can be understood intuitively at a glance. Neither extreme is possible to achieve so it is best to attempt both.*

Choreographed explosions are fun. While I know near to nothing about explosions I'm decent at throwing together electronics.
